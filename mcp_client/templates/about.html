What is Intake Triage?

Intake Triage is a smart system that helps organizations handle customer issues, complaints, and inquiries. Instead of manually reading and sorting each request, our AI-powered system automatically understands your issue, determines how urgent it is, and sends it to the right team.

4

  • Classification: The AI reads your issue and determines what type of problem it is (billing, technical, complaint, etc.)
  • Severity Scoring: Based on keywords and context, it assigns a priority level from 1 (low) to 5 (urgent)
  • Smart Routing: Your issue is automatically directed to the team best equipped to help you
